Formatting | Content

  • use jake's resume template found on overleaf
  • use consistent formatting (dates, location, job title and company)
  • verb tone consistency matters, use past tense for every bullet, you may use present tense for current ones
  • don't bury your best accomplishments
  • coherent order for project/experience (chronological) and bullets (impact first)
  • numbers in bullets are a great way of demonstrating scale and impact
  • education - put your degree + university with dates, highschool (and highschool grades) is generally irrelevant as it's implied you went to highschool and got good grades, especially if you're at a top university
  • work experience - put any relevant work experience or even projects/clubs that required substantial responsibilities. include bullet points demonstrating scale, impact and responsibilities
  • projects - projects that highlight leadership, design or even impact of things you've worked on like websites, games, club, or build projects (ideally field co-related, but not required)
  • technical skills - programming languages, frameworks, developer tools, spoken languages and anything related to the assets you have related to the field
  • awards - any awards that show you excel in a field, can be totally unrelated to the job's field
